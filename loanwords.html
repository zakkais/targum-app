Loanwords

Words that Hebrew and Aramaic borrowed from the languages Jews lived among. Greek words came in during the Hellenistic period, Latin words under Roman rule, and Persian words from the days of the Babylonian exile and the Persian empire. The French entries are Rashi's לעזים (laazim): Old French glosses he used to explain difficult words in the Chumash.

Every entry below is attested: each one gives the source it comes from and where the word appears.
